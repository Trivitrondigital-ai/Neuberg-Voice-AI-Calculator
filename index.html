    Month-by-month model
    How the model works
    1. Once live, Voice AI answers the routine share of inbound calls and makes the automatable share of outbound calls.
    2. Containment = target containment × bot skill × CRM readiness. CRM readiness is adoption divided by the gate, capped at 100%. Bot skill starts at 25% at go-live and reaches 100% after the ramp months.
    3. Calls the bot cannot resolve go to an agent. Handle time on those calls falls by the handover saving, scaled by CRM readiness.
    4. Billing rounds every call up to a whole pulse, so the model adds half a pulse per call on average. Escalated calls are billed for their AI portion.
    5. Agent need scales with human talk minutes. Only the realisation share of freed capacity becomes a saving. Team leads follow the span of control.
    6. New bookings come from abandoned calls the bot now answers (at today's conversion), the conversion uplift on booking enquiries, and proactive outbound calls. The benefit counts contribution margin, not revenue.
    7. One-time costs are spread over the months before go-live. Bot trainers are paid from month 1. The platform fee starts at go-live.
    8. Voice CSAT blends AI-resolved calls at AI CSAT with all other calls at today's CSAT plus the queue-relief lift.
    9. The comparison line holds CRM adoption at today's level for all 36 months.
